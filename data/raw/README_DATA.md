# Raw data notes

All CSVs in this folder use `|` as the delimiter. The non-seed resource and news files are live scraper outputs. Files ending `_seed.csv` are initial records assembled from publicly indexed Samfya Town Council pages/documents and are retained as a fallback and comparison set. None of these files is the final cleaned dataset.

Blank fields mean the source did not expose that value; do not invent them. Preserve the direct source URLs and retrieval timestamps during cleaning.
